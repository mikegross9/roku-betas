Channel 3 & The Million Point Question

Two small private Roku apps by Mike Gross, shared with friends as beta channels.

About Privacy Terms

About

These are hobby apps built to be played from the couch with a standard Roku remote. They're not for sale and aren't listed in the Roku Streaming Store. You get them by entering an access code on your Roku account.

Arcade Channel 3

A channel-flip TV menu of twelve couch games, from Snake and Spud Wars to STOMP and Hot Potato. Some games have cartoon slapstick violence.

Quiz show The Million Point Question

Fifteen questions, one ladder, four lifelines and a host with opinions. Play alone or pass the remote around.

Privacy

Neither app collects, stores or sends any personal information.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • No purchases or payments of any kind.
  • The apps don't connect to the internet while you play. High scores and settings stay on your Roku device.

Your Roku device and Roku account are covered by Roku's own privacy policy, which these apps don't change.

Terms of use
